Ultra simple baby tracking. One tap logs a feed, a pee or poop diaper, or sleep. One glance answers the 3am question: when did she last eat, and which side.

FOUR BUTTONS THAT NEVER MOVE
Feed (left, right, bottle), Pee, Poop, Sleep. Prefer Wet and Dirty? One setting renames them. One tap logs it now. A long press fixes the time, the side, the bottle amount or the stool colour. Undo sits in a toast for a few seconds. No confirmation sheets, ever.

WITHOUT OPENING THE APP
Lock screen and home screen widgets that log with one tap. An Apple Watch app and a complication that shows how long since the last feed. A Live Activity on the lock screen while a feed or a sleep is running. Siri and the Action button.

BOTH PARENTS, NO ACCOUNTS
Log together: your partner, a grandparent or a nanny scans a code from your phone and joins the same log from their own iPhone. You all tap the same four buttons and see the same list, so "did you already change her?" answers itself. There is no account to create, and no server of ours in the middle: the records live on your devices and in your own iCloud.

THE FIRST WEEKS
The tally sheet the hospital sends home, as a screen: your logged diapers by day of life, beside a breastfeeding reference for the first two weeks from NHS Healthier Together, with the "call your pediatrician if" lines under the table. The reference is general guidance, not a target or a verdict about your baby.

THE STAIN HELPER
Tell it once what you have at home, and a blowout, spit-up, breast milk or formula stain gets the steps in order, skipping anything you do not own.

FREE, AND STAYING FREE
Logging, the first-weeks tally, full history, widgets, the Apple Watch app, the Live Activity, logging together, the stain helper and more than one baby. Nothing you have already logged is ever locked later.

BABY+, OPTIONAL
- The pediatrician summary: a one-page PDF since the last visit, with daily feeds, wet and dirty counts, sleep totals, the longest stretch and weights
- Trends: feeds a day, diaper counts, and the longest sleep stretch over the weeks
- CSV export of every entry

NO ADS, NO AI
No ads, of any kind. No AI panels or predictions. The four buttons will not move in an update.
