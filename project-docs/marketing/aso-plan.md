Updated September 11, 2026. No ASC record yet. Evidence and method are in
`marketreport.md` and `research/`.

`diaper tracker`, `simple baby tracker`, `nursing timer`, `baby feeding` and
`twins tracker` are at Astro's floor. Search will not launch this app; it has to
rank on the long tail and win through channels (see `marketreport.md` section 7).

## Metadata

- Name candidates: `Baby Tracker: Feeds & Diapers`, `Baby Log: Feed, Diaper, Sleep`.
  Check name availability in ASC before committing.
- Subtitle candidates: `First Week Diaper Count`, `Doctor-Ready Newborn Log`.
- Keyword pool: `newborn,diaper,feeding,breastfeeding,nursing,pumping,log,sleep,wet,dirty,pediatrician,timer,watch,widget,twins`
- Category: to decide (Health & Fitness or Lifestyle). Not a Kids Category app.

The name carries the category noun. The subtitle carries the differentiator,
because the name cannot.

## Product guardrail

The product is not a renamed counter. A reviewer on a fresh install must reach,
without a purchase or days of data:

```text
one tap logs a feed, wet, dirty, or sleep
        ↓
the first-week tally against the typical range for this day of life
        ↓
"Fed 2h 14m ago, Left" on the Now card, lock screen, and Watch
        ↓
a preview of the pediatrician summary
```

Screenshots and review notes must demonstrate this sequence (4.3 lesson from
Protein and Caffeine).

## Screenshot messages

Use one period-free header with no prices, discounts, or `free` claims:

1. The first-week diaper count
2. When she last ate, and which side
3. One tap from your wrist
4. Both parents, no accounts
5. Ready for the pediatrician

## Claim language

Use `typical range`, `general guidance`, and `call your pediatrician if`. Never
say `normal`, `abnormal`, `healthy`, `dehydrated`, or anything that reads as a
diagnosis (1.4.1, 1.1.6).
