# Error sweep of the older localized metadata, 2026-11-20

Scope: the 40 `fastlane/metadata/` locales not rewritten in [2026-11-20-l10n.md](2026-11-20-l10n.md) (every locale except en-US, it, pt-BR, es-MX, es-ES, en-CA, fr-CA, de-DE, fr-FR, en-GB). Outright errors only; style and keyword strategy untouched. Nothing was sent to App Store Connect (`asc` is not installed here; `scripts/aso-check-locales.py <locales>` was run instead).

Scan results: no `%(dias)s`-style or other template leaks, no field over its limit, no empty field, no competitor names, no emojis, no treat/cure/prevent claims (every description says the app does not diagnose or treat), no wrong-script text, all descriptions have the same 17 paragraphs and the disclaimer. "todo" (pt-PT, Portuguese for "all") and "KNAPPER" (da, no, Danish and Norwegian for "buttons") are false positives.

## Not judged without a native speaker

- Indic descriptions (bn, gu, hi, kn, ml, mr, or, pa, ta, te) and ur-PK, ar-SA, he, th: checked for leaks, English left over, script and structure only, not for fluency.
- zh-Hant `條碼` for the QR code (zh-Hans says `二维码`); `QR 碼` may read better in Taiwan.
- ru `Аккаунта Apple` (Apple's own ru text may say `учётной записи Apple`; uk uses `облікового запису Apple`).
- Many locales switch between singular "you" (phone, partner) and plural "you" (devices, iCloud) in the same paragraph (ca, da, el, hr, hu, nl, no, pl, ro, sv, ...). It reads as "you" then "you two parents" and comes from the source, so it was left.
- Keyword left in place: vi `sinh đôi` (twins) shares the syllable `sinh` with the subtitle; ur-PK `بچوں کا ڈاکٹر` shares the particle `کا` with the subtitle. The repo checker flags both; neither is a real repeat.
- el, ru, uk, pl: the subtitle repeats the name's main word (Θηλασμός/πάνες/ύπνος, кормление/годування, Karmienie). That is strategy, not an error, so it was left.
- Descriptions in these 40 locales are the older, shorter text (no reminders, pump, custom buttons) and link the privacy policy at `jackwallner.github.io/baby/...`, while en-US links `jackwallner.com/ios/baby/...`. URLs were not touched.
