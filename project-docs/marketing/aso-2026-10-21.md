## Recommendations

1. **en-US keywords: swap `nursing` for `nap,shared`** (94 to 97 chars).
   The `nursing` results are all nursing-school apps, so it earns nothing for a
   baby tracker. `nap` extends sleep searches. `shared` pairs with the name
   for "shared baby tracker", which describes Log together, the main
   difference from Huckleberry and the other top results, and where the app
   shows nowhere today.

   `breastfeeding,bottle,sleep,nap,pee,poop,wet,dirty,timer,infant,formula,twins,watch,feeding,shared`

   en-CA, en-GB and en-AU carry the same `nursing` and get the same swap.
   Keep the extra term each one already has (`pediatrician`, `nap`, `GP`).

2. **Keep the name and subtitle.** "Baby Tracker: Feeds & Diapers" holds 28 for
   the category's biggest term. "Newborn Log & Diaper Count" ranks 8 for
   "diaper count". Changing either would put working ranks at risk for
   unmeasured ones.

3. **What's New for a resubmitted 1.4** (the live text describes build 51 only):

   > Feed reminders: pick a gap from 2 to 4 hours and get a nudge with the
   > side to start on. History shows Today and Yesterday at the top, and Baby+
   > adds a chart you can drag through to see any day. Logging together is
   > faster and steadier: a partner's entries show up in moments, never twice,
   > and nothing on the screen jumps when you tap. Plus clearer joining, more
   > accurate doctor reports, and better VoiceOver support.

   It needs the same change in all 50 locales at submission.

4. **Promotional text: leave it.** It is not indexed, and the current line
   ("Free forever... the four buttons never move") already states what sets
   the app apart.

5. **Not recommended:** `pumping` and `breast milk` (popular, but those results
   are pumping and milk-stash apps, and the app does not track pumping), and
   `poop tracker` alone (adult gut-health results).
