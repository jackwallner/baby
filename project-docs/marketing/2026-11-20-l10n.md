# Localized metadata from keyword research, 2026-11-20

Nine locales rewritten: it, pt-BR, es-MX, es-ES, en-CA, fr-CA, de-DE, fr-FR, en-GB.
Nothing was pushed to App Store Connect. Not touched: en-US, URLs, release notes,
screenshots, the other 41 locales.

Method: Apple search hints per storefront (autocomplete), iTunes Search API
(top 50 per term, our `trackId` 6811133796 for rank), competitor product pages
for subtitles. Searches ran 2026-11-20. Competitor brand names are not used in
any field.

## What changed everywhere

- Apple's localization table lists en-GB as a secondary locale for Italy,
  Brazil, Mexico, Spain, Germany, Austria, Switzerland, France and Australia.
  So English words ("baby tracker", "timer", "watch", "twins", "formula",
  "shared") already reach those storefronts through en-GB. The local
  keyword fields no longer spend bytes on them (the old fields carried `watch`
  and `timer` in every locale). en-GB keeps those English terms and adds
  `diaper`, the word those storefronts type.
- Canada indexes en-CA plus fr-CA; the US indexes es-MX, pt-BR and fr-FR
  as secondary. Spanish and Portuguese terms in es-MX and pt-BR therefore also
  serve US Hispanic and Brazilian-diaspora searches, and US English is
  covered by en-US.
- Descriptions are rewritten from the current en-US feature list (All button,
  Wet/Dirty rename, reminders, Action button, stain helper, free versus Baby+).
  The old ones predated those features. Subscription, disclaimer and legal
  paragraphs are the live text, unchanged. No medical claims; the disclaimer
  stays.
- Button names follow the app's own catalog (`Shared/Localizable.xcstrings`):
  Tutti, Todos, Alle, Bagnato/Sporco, Molhada/Suja, Mojado/Sucio, Nass/Voll.
  The app ships en, de, es, it and pt-BR; the fr and en-GB/CA listings keep
  the line saying the app is in English.

## it (Italy; also Switzerland)

Autocomplete: `poppate` gives "diario delle poppate", "conta poppate"; `pannolini`
gives "registro pannolini del neonato", "diario pannolini"; `neonato` gives
"diario neonato", "monitoraggio neonato", "sonno neonato"; `allattamento`
gives "diario allattamento"; `baby` gives "baby tracker".
Competitors (title / subtitle): Il mio bebè - Allattamento (4,798 ratings),
Baby Tracker - Diario neonato (917; "Sonno, poppate e pannolini"), Diario Neonato
(134), Bebè + (2,127). Thin: `diario pannolini` (top results have 0 to 917 ratings).

- Name kept (autocomplete shows "neonato: poppate e pannolini"; matches
  `poppate`, `pannolini`, `neonato` demand).
- Subtitle: `Diario semplice per il bebè` to `Diario allattamento e sonno`
  (diario + allattamento + sonno were all autocomplete terms; the old
  subtitle's "semplice" and "per" were wasted).
- Keywords: `biberon,pipì,cacca,pediatra,bebè,bimbo,registro,contatore,gemelli,nanna,genitori,condiviso,gratis` (97).
  `bebè` (autocomplete "tracker bebè"), `contatore` and `registro` (counter /
  log forms), `nanna` (autocomplete "nanna"). Dropped `watch,timer` (en-GB
  covers them), `latte,seno,materno,lattante` (weak, no autocomplete).

`couches` and `boires` return nothing. Competitors: Baby Tracker - Carnet de bébé

- Name kept: `boires` is the Quebec word for a feed, `couches` the word for diapers.
- Subtitle: `Journal simple du nouveau-né` to `Allaitement, biberon, sommeil`
  (the three terms with real autocomplete; matches how top competitors write theirs).
- Keywords: `nouveau-né,journal,carnet,poupon,pipi,caca,pédiatre,sieste,jumeaux,minuterie,lait,partagé,gratuit` (97).
  `poupon` (Quebec for newborn), `carnet` (competitors say "carnet de bébé"),
  `nouveau-né` moved from the subtitle. `watch` dropped (en-CA has it).
  Weakest evidence of the nine; the CA proceeds justify keeping it, and fr-CA
  is only a secondary index for the rest of Canada.
