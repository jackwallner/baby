Ultra simple baby tracking. One tap logs a feed, a pee or poop diaper, or sleep. One glance answers the 3am question: when did the baby last eat, and which side to start on.

FOUR BUTTONS THAT NEVER MOVE
Feed, Pee, Poop, Sleep. Prefer Wet and Dirty? One setting renames them. One tap logs it now, and a Side row (Left, Right, Bottle) opens under Feed for a moment if you want to add it. A long press fixes the time, the side, the bottle amount or the stool colour. Undo sits at the top of the screen for a few seconds. No confirmation sheets, ever.

FORGOT TO TAP?
Wind the log time back in five-minute steps and the next tap logs at that time. Add an older entry from the bottom of the screen to backfill the first days.

REMINDERS YOU CHOOSE
A nudge when the next feed is due, counted from when the last one started, every 2 to 4 hours as you set it, with the side to start on. The evening before a newborn checkup, a note that your summary for the doctor is ready. Both are optional and stay on your phone.

THE FIRST WEEKS
The tally sheet the hospital sends home, as a screen: your logged diapers by day of life, beside a breastfeeding reference for the first two weeks from NHS Healthier Together, with the "call your pediatrician if" lines under it. The reference is general guidance, not a target or a verdict about your baby.

THE STAIN HELPER
Tell it once what you have at home, and a blowout, spit-up, breast milk or formula stain gets the steps in order, skipping anything you do not own.

FREE, AND STAYING FREE
Logging, feed reminders, the first-weeks tally, full history, the stain helper, every appearance including a warm Night light for dark rooms, and more than one baby. Nothing you have already logged is ever locked later.

BABY+, OPTIONAL
- The pediatrician summary: a PDF of the days since the last visit to share or print, with daily feeds and the longest gap between them, wet and dirty counts, sleep, weights and your notes
- Trends: feeds a day, diaper counts and sleep over the weeks
- CSV export of every entry

Baby+ is available as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase that never renews. New subscribers may get a free trial. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled; manage or cancel them in Google Play under Payments and subscriptions. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.

NO ADS, NO AI, NO ACCOUNT
No ads of any kind, no AI panels or predictions, and no account to create. Your log stays on your phone.

Baby Tracker is a log, not medical advice. It does not diagnose, treat or assess your baby, and it is not a medical device. Call your pediatrician with any concern.
