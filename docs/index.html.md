Logging, the first-weeks tally, history, the widgets, the Watch
app, partner sharing and the stain helper are free and stay
free. The buttons stay where you learned them. Nothing you

## What does Baby+ add?

Baby+ turns the log since the last visit into a single page:
feeds a day, wet and dirty counts, sleep totals, the longest
stretch, and weights. Hand it over or AirDrop it in the exam
room. It also adds trends and CSV export.

The page itself is on screen before you buy anything, with a
worked example until you have logged a day.

Two in the morning

## How does the stain helper work?

Tell it once what you have at home. A blowout, spit-up, breast
milk or formula then gets the steps in order, skipping anything
you do not own, starting with the one that matters: cold water,
from the back, and do not rub.

Without opening the app

## Can I log from Apple Watch or the Lock Screen?

Yes. Log from Apple Watch, a lock screen or home screen widget, Siri,
or the Action button while you are holding the baby with the
other arm. A Live Activity counts a running feed or sleep on the
lock screen, and the complication shows how long since the last
feed.

Typical ranges are general guidance, not medical advice. Call
your pediatrician with any concern.

## Frequently asked questions

## What is the best baby tracker app for feeds and diapers?

Baby Tracker: Feeds & Diapers is a simple option built around four buttons: Feed, Wet, Dirty and Sleep. One tap logs each one, and the Now card shows when the baby last ate and on which side.

## Is there a free baby tracker app I can share with my partner?

Yes. Logging, full history, widgets, the Apple Watch app and sharing with a partner are free. Your partner, a grandparent or a nanny joins the same log from their own iPhone by scanning a code, and there is no account to create.

## Does the baby tracker work on Apple Watch?

Yes. The Watch app logs a feed, diaper or sleep with a tap, and the complication shows how long it has been since the last feed. You can also log from lock screen and home screen widgets, Siri and the Action button.

## Can I use it for twins or more than one baby?

Yes. Support for more than one baby is free, so each baby keeps their own log.

## How do I track newborn wet and dirty diapers in the first week?

Log each diaper with one tap on Wet or Dirty. For the first two weeks, today's counts appear beside the typical range for your baby's day of life, which is general guidance and not medical advice.

## Does it have ads, accounts or AI?

No. There are no ads, no AI predictions and no account to create. Your records live on your devices and in your own iCloud.

## What does the optional Baby+ upgrade add?

Baby+ adds a one-page pediatrician summary since the last visit, trends over the weeks and CSV export. Nothing you have already logged is ever locked. Prices are shown in the app before you buy and vary by region.
