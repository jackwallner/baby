Help and contact

## Support

For help, email
[jackwallner@gmail.com](mailto:jackwallner@gmail.com).
Include the device model and iOS or watchOS version when reporting a
problem.

## How do two parents log together?

The person who started the log opens More, then Invite someone, and
shows the QR code. On the other iPhone, install Baby Tracker, choose
Join a shared log, and scan the code (or scan it with the Camera app,
or open the link sent in a message). Both phones then log into the
same baby.

## How do two Android phones log together?

On the first phone open Settings, then Invite someone, and tap
Continue with Google. Read out the code or tap Send invite link. On
the other phone install Baby Tracker, choose Join a shared log, enter
the code (or open the link) and tap Join with Google. An iPhone can't
join an Android log yet. To stop, choose Leave shared log; see
[deleting your sharing data](https://jackwallner.com/ios/baby/delete-account.html).

## My partner does not see my entries

Both phones need an internet connection and to be signed in to
iCloud. Open More in the app: Log together shows who has joined. If
no one is listed, join again from the other phone. An offline phone
catches up when it reconnects.

## I logged something by mistake

Tap Undo right after logging, or open the history, swipe the entry,
and delete it.

## Restore a purchase

Open Settings and select Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple
Account that made the purchase.

## A question about my baby's health

The app shows typical ranges as general guidance only. Call your
pediatrician with any concern about feeding, diapers, or sleep.
