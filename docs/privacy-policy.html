Privacy Policy

Baby Tracker keeps your baby's records on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We do not operate an account system, sell personal information, show ads, or use advertising trackers.

What you log

Feeds, diapers, sleep, and any notes you add are stored on your device. They are not sent to our servers, because there are none, and they are not sent to RevenueCat or advertisers.

Logging together

If you invite another caregiver, the shared baby's records are stored in Apple's iCloud under the Apple Account of the person who started the log and are visible to everyone who joins. An invite is a private link, shown as a QR code or sent as a message: anyone holding that link can join and edit the log, so share it only with people you trust. You can remove someone or stop sharing at any time. Apple handles that storage under its own privacy policy.

Camera

If you choose to scan an invite code, the camera is used only to read that code on your device. No image is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, product identifiers, entitlement status, and purchase events so the app can provide access to paid features. RevenueCat does not receive anything you log.

Notifications

If enabled, reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Data deletion

You can delete any entry in the app, stop sharing to remove a partner's access, and delete the app to remove local data. Purchase records remain subject to Apple's policies.
