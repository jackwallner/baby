# Delete your sharing sign-in | Baby Tracker

> How to delete the Google sign-in and shared data Baby Tracker's Log together uses on Android.

Web page: https://jackwallner.github.io/baby/delete-account.html

Baby Tracker for Android

## Delete your sharing sign-in and data

Baby Tracker has no account of its own. On Android, Log together uses
a Google sign-in to keep a shared log in Google Firebase for the
people you invite. You can delete that sign-in and your shared data
at any time.

## In the app

Open Settings, then Invite someone or leave, then Leave shared log.
This deletes your sign-in from Baby Tracker's sharing service and
removes you from the shared log. Everything logged so far stays on
your phone. If you are the last person in the shared log, every baby
and entry in it is deleted from Firebase as well. If a leave did not
finish deleting the sign-in, Settings shows Delete sharing sign-in.

## By email

If you no longer have the app, email
[jackwallner@gmail.com](mailto:jackwallner@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20Baby%20Tracker%20sharing%20data)
from the Google account you signed in with. We delete the sign-in and
your membership within 30 days and confirm by email.

## What is deleted and what is kept

Deleted: your Firebase sign-in (name, email address and user id from
Google) and your membership in the shared log. Entries you logged stay
in the shared log for the people still in it, because they are part
of their baby's record, until the last person leaves. Nothing is kept
after the last person leaves. Purchases are handled by Google Play and
RevenueCat and are not affected.
