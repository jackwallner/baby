# Privacy Policy | Baby Tracker

> Privacy policy for the Baby Tracker feeds and diapers app by Jack Wallner.

Web page: https://jackwallner.com/ios/baby/privacy-policy.html

Effective October 21, 2026

## Privacy Policy

Baby Tracker keeps your baby's records on your devices and in your
own iCloud account. On Android, an optional shared log uses Google
Firebase, described below. We do not operate an account system, sell
personal information, show ads, or use advertising trackers.

## What you log

Feeds, diapers, sleep, and any notes you add are stored on your
device. Unless you turn on logging together on Android (below), they
are not sent to any server we operate, and they are never sent to
RevenueCat or advertisers.

## Logging together

If you invite another caregiver, the shared baby's records are stored
in Apple's iCloud under the Apple Account of the person who started
the log and are visible to everyone who joins. An invite is a private
link, shown as a QR code or sent as a message: anyone holding that
link can join and edit the log, so share it only with people you
trust. You can remove someone or stop sharing at any time. Apple
handles that storage under its own privacy policy.

## Camera

If you choose to scan an invite code, the camera is used only to read
that code on your device. No image is recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

## Purchases

Apple processes purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user
identifier, product identifiers, entitlement status, and purchase
events so the app can provide access to paid features. RevenueCat
does not receive anything you log.

## Notifications

If enabled, reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

## On Android

The Android app keeps the log in the app's private storage on your
phone. Android's own backup may copy it to your Google account if you
have device backup turned on; we never receive it. Google Play
processes purchases, and RevenueCat receives the same purchase
information described above. Reminders are scheduled locally on the
phone.

## Logging together on Android

Logging together on Android is off until you choose to start or join
a shared log. When you do, you sign in with Google, and Google
Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, operated by
Google for us) stores your name, email address and a user id from
your Google account, plus a copy of every baby and entry on your
phone: names, birth dates, feeds, sides, bottle amounts, diapers,
stool colours, sleep, weights and notes. Everyone in the shared log
can see and edit them, and only they can: our security rules refuse
everyone else. Other members see your first name. An invite is a
short code or link that lets anyone holding it join for seven days,
so share it only with people you trust. Data is encrypted in transit.
We do not use it for anything else, and Firebase does not show ads or
run analytics in this app. An iPhone cannot join an Android shared
log yet.

Leaving a shared log deletes your Google sign-in from the sharing
service and your membership; the log stays on your phone. When the
last person leaves, every baby and entry in the shared log is deleted
from Firebase. See
[how to delete your sharing data](https://jackwallner.com/ios/baby/delete-account.html),
including by email.

## Data deletion

You can delete any entry in the app, stop sharing to remove a
partner's access, leave an Android shared log to delete your sharing
sign-in, and delete the app to remove local data. Purchase
records remain subject to Apple's policies.
