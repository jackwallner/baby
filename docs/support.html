Help and contact

Support

For help, email jackwallner@gmail.com. Include the device model and iOS or watchOS version when reporting a problem.

My partner does not see my entries

Both of you need to be signed in to iCloud and to have accepted the share invitation. Open Settings in the app to check who the baby is shared with.

I logged something by mistake

Tap Undo right after logging, or open the history, swipe the entry, and delete it.

Restore a purchase

Open Settings and select Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase.

A question about my baby's health

The app shows typical ranges as general guidance only. Call your pediatrician with any concern about feeding, diapers, or sleep.
